A funded research project at the Universidad Autónoma de Bucaramanga (UNAB) that developed a methodology to foster learning of computer programming through gamification, together with a mobile application to help students and teachers follow the state of their learning.

The project informed a pedagogical diagnosis and restructuring of the computer programming courses in the Systems Engineering programme.
